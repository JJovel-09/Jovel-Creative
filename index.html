How we work together

Four steps, from the first conversation to the files landing in your hands.

1

Tell us what's not working

Describe the part of the week that costs you the most time. No spec, no software terms, no need to know what the answer is.

2

We map how you work

We go through your current steps, numbers and documents, then agree on what the tool needs to do before anything gets built.

3

We build and test it

The tool is built in software you already use, then tested against real examples from your business so the answers hold up.

4

You get the files and a walkthrough

You get the editable files plus a walkthrough of how to use and change them. We adjust it until it fits.

How this stays straightforward

You own the files

The editable files are yours to keep, change and pass to someone else. Nothing is locked behind a subscription.

Built in software you already use

Tools are built to run in the spreadsheet and document software already on your machine. No new platform to learn.

Scope agreed before building

What the tool does, and what it costs, is settled before the work starts. You approve the plan first.

Built for one workflow

Every tool is made for how your business runs. Nothing is resold from a template library.
