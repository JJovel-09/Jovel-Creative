How we work together

Four steps, from the first conversation to the files landing in your hands.

1

Tell us what's not working

Describe the part of the week that costs you the most time. No spec, no software terms, no need to know what the answer is.

2

We map how you work

We go through your current steps, numbers and documents, then agree on what the tool needs to do and which platform it is built in.

3

We build and test it

The tool is built in the platform we agreed on, then tested against real examples from your business so the answers hold up.

4

You get the files and a walkthrough

You get the editable files plus a walkthrough of how to use and change them. We adjust it until it fits.

How this stays straightforward

Fixed price before work begins

What the tool does, and what it costs, is settled and approved before anything is built. The number does not move partway through.

Built around your workflow

Built around your workflow, not a one-size-fits-all template. We may reuse our own internal methods, but the tool is shaped to how your business runs.

Human reviewed before delivery

Every file is checked by a person against real examples from your business before it reaches you. Nothing is handed over unread.

Editable and yours after full payment

Once the project is paid in full, the editable files are yours to keep, change and pass to someone else. Nothing is locked behind a subscription.

Few meetings, only useful ones

We meet when a conversation genuinely moves the build forward. The rest is handled in writing, on your schedule.
