How fast will I get my kit?
Most kits are delivered within 24–48 hours after you confirm the details and package. Rush delivery (guaranteed 24-hour turnaround) is available for an additional $29.

One round of revisions is included with every package. Additional rounds are $25 each.

I host the landing page for you. You get a clean short link and matching QR code. No setup or technical knowledge needed on your end.

You own all the files. Restyling an existing kit for a new listing is available at a reduced rate — just ask when you request.

Payment is due before delivery. I accept Venmo, Zelle, and PayPal. I’ll include payment instructions in my reply after you submit the request form.
