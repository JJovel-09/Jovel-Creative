About Jovel Creative

Fast, professional marketing kits that help realtors get more attention, capture more leads, and look great doing it.

Where It Started

I spent more than a decade shaping communication strategies, building digital content, and helping organizations tell their story clearly. Over time I noticed something: realtors and local businesses put serious effort into their listings and events, but their promotional materials rarely matched the quality of their work.

A great listing deserves great promotion.

Jovel Creative was built on that idea: give hardworking realtors a fast, complete marketing kit that looks sharp, captures leads, and is ready to go in 24 hours.

My Background

My career has always centered on communication, design direction, and digital strategy. I have written for leaders, built internal platforms, shaped public messaging, and helped brands connect with their audience in ways that feel clear and honest.

What I learned from all that experience: clarity drives action.

When a flyer is clean, the message is obvious, and there is one clear next step — people respond. That principle drives every kit I build.

Why I Built This Service

Most realtors do not have time to design a flyer, resize it for social, build a landing page, and set up a QR code — especially when a listing goes live or an open house is days away. They need it done right, fast, and without a back-and-forth that takes a week.

That is exactly what I solve.

One request. One fixed price. A complete kit in 24 hours.

How It Works

Simple, fast, and built around your schedule.

1

You submit the request form

Tell me about your listing or event: address, key details, your logo or headshot, and the action you want people to take (RSVP, book a tour, claim an offer).

2

I confirm and start your kit

I reply within a few hours to confirm details and package. Once you approve, I get to work building your flyer, social graphics, QR code, and landing page.

3

Your kit is delivered in 24–48 hours

You get all files by email: print-ready PDF flyer, web-ready PNG social graphics, a tracked QR code, and a live lead-capture landing page link. One round of revisions included.

What You Get With Every Kit

  • ✓ A primary marketing flyer built for print and digital sharing
  • ✓ 3 social media graphics sized for feed posts and stories
  • ✓ A QR code that links to your listing or event page
  • ✓ A lead-capture landing page with RSVP, booking, or offer claim (Standard & Premium)
  • ✓ A 30-second AI video invite (Premium)
  • ✓ 24–48 hour turnaround with one revision round included
