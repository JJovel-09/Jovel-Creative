Services Offered

Marketing Kit Services

Jovel Creative provides fixed-price marketing kit services for real estate professionals, including:

  • Custom promotional flyer design (print-ready PDF and digital formats)
  • Social media graphics (square post, vertical story/reel, and banner sizes)
  • QR code generation linked to your listing, landing page, or URL
  • Lead-capture landing page design and hosting (Standard and Premium kits)
  • 30-second AI video invite production (Premium kit)
  • Optional add-ons as listed on the Packages page

Kit Process

Every kit order follows this process:

  • Request - Submit the kit request form with your listing details and package selection
  • Confirmation - I reply by email within a few hours to confirm details and package
  • Production - I design and build all kit components
  • Delivery - All files delivered by email within the stated turnaround window
  • Revisions - One round of revisions included per kit

What's Not Included

Unless specifically agreed upon in writing:

  • Professional photography or videography
  • Logo or brand identity design
  • Copywriting or listing description writing
  • Ongoing monthly retainer services (available upon request)
  • Changes after the included revision round (billed separately)

Not sure what you need? Reach out before ordering and I'll point you to the right package.

  • Any brokerage brand colors or style preferences

Delays in providing details will extend the turnaround window. No photos? I can source stock imagery.

Feedback
